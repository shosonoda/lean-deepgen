  • LeanDeepgen.Tradeoff.balancing

Balancing principle. Let \(S \subseteq \mathbb R\) be a set of depths on which \(\bias \ge 0\) is nonincreasing and \(\var (\cdot ,n) \ge 0\) is nondecreasing. If \(k_0 \in S\) balances the two terms, \(\bias (k_0) = \var (k_0,n)\), then \(\gen (k,n) \ge \bias (k_0)\) for every \(k \in S\), while \(\gen (k_0,n) = 2\bias (k_0)\); hence \(k_0\) minimizes the bound over \(S\) up to the factor \(2\).

Proof ▶

Case split on \(k \le k_0\) or \(k \ge k_0\): in the first case \(\bias (k) \ge \bias (k_0)\) and \(\var (k,n) \ge 0\); in the second \(\var (k,n) \ge \var (k_0,n) = \bias (k_0)\) and \(\bias (k) \ge 0\). The identity at \(k_0\) is the balance equation.

  • LeanDeepgen.Tradeoff.tradeoff_PP

PP regime (??tab:tradeoff). Let \(\beta ,\gamma {\gt} 0\) and \(n {\gt} 0\). At the balancing depth \(\kopt = n^{1/(2\beta +\gamma )}\), \(\gen (\kopt ,n) = \kopt ^{-\beta } + \sqrt{\kopt ^{\gamma }/n} = 2\, n^{-\beta /(2\beta +\gamma )}\), and for every depth \(k {\gt} 0\), \(\gen (k,n) = k^{-\beta } + \sqrt{k^{\gamma }/n} \ge n^{-\beta /(2\beta +\gamma )}\). Thus \(\kopt \) is optimal up to the factor \(2\) and \(\gen (\kopt ,n) \asymp n^{-\beta /(2\beta +\gamma )}\).

Proof ▶

Both terms equal \(n^{-\beta /(2\beta +\gamma )}\) at \(\kopt \) (??lem:bias-poly-kpp, ??lem:var-poly-kpp), so the balancing principle ??lem:balancing on \(S = (0,\infty )\) gives both claims.

Ordering note, first half. If \(\gamma \le 1\) then the EP rate is no worse than the PL rate: \(n^{-1/2}(\log n)^{\gamma /2} = O(\sqrt{\log n/n})\).

Proof ▶

For \(\log n \ge 1\), \((\log n)^{\gamma /2} \le (\log n)^{1/2}\).
