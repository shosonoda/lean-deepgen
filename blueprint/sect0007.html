\[ \hat{\mathfrak R}_S(G) \ge \frac{a_1 - a_0}{2}. \]

(This is lem:shatter-rademacher with the boundedness of \(G\) on the sample replaced by the weaker hypothesis on the Rademacher averages; the hypothesis \(a_0 \le a_1\) is not used in the proof.)

Proof ▼

Step 1. For a sign pattern \(\sigma \) let \(\psi (\sigma ) := \frac1n \sum _i \sigma _i a_{c_i}\) with \(c_i = 1\) exactly when \(\sigma _i = 1\). The labeling \(c\) is realized by some \(g \in G\), so \(\psi (\sigma ) \le \sup _{g \in G} \frac1n \sum _i \sigma _i g(x_i)\).

Step 2. \(\psi (\sigma ) + \psi (-\sigma ) = a_1 - a_0\): the \(i\)-th terms add up to \(a_1 - a_0\) for either value of \(\sigma _i\).

Step 3. Reindexing by \(\sigma \mapsto -\sigma \), \(2 \sum _\sigma \psi (\sigma ) = \sum _\sigma (\psi (\sigma ) + \psi (-\sigma )) = 2^n (a_1 - a_0)\).

Step 4. Average the inequality of Step 1 over the sign patterns.
